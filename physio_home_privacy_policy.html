Privacy Policy for Physio Home

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Physio Home ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected through the Physio Home mobile application, how it is used, and when it may be shared.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Physio Home may collect the following information:

  • Account information: information associated with your account, such as your name, email address, and phone number, and authentication information provided through Google, email, or phone sign-in.
  • Health information: health conditions, injuries, and other information you choose to provide about your physical condition.
  • Exercise and progress information: exercise activity, plans, records, and progress information entered or generated while using the app.
  • Physical information: age, weight, and height when you choose to provide them.
  • Technical information: information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app, which may be processed by the infrastructure providers used by the app.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to create and manage accounts, provide app features, support physiotherapy follow-up, display exercise plans and progress, communicate with users about account or service matters, maintain app security, and troubleshoot technical problems.

3. Sharing with Authorized Healthcare Professionals

Health, exercise, progress, and physical information may be made available to healthcare professionals who are authorized within Physio Home to access the relevant patient's information for care and follow-up. The app is intended to restrict this access to authorized healthcare-professional accounts; ordinary users and unrelated doctors should not have access.

Please do not enter information that you do not want to share with the healthcare professionals authorized to view your account.

4. Service Providers

Physio Home uses Firebase services for app functionality, including account authentication and data-related services. Information may be processed by Google/Firebase as a service provider under its applicable terms and privacy practices. We do not use the app to serve advertising or perform advertising analytics, and the app does not request location or camera access, based on the information provided to us.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Access to health information should be controlled through appropriate account permissions and Firebase security rules.

6. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service and for legitimate operational or legal purposes. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information, contact us at the email address below. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request. Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

Physio Home is not intended to be used by children without the involvement and appropriate consent of a parent or legal guardian. If you believe a child has provided personal information without the required consent, please contact us so we can review the request.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after an updated policy becomes available means the updated policy applies to your use of the app.
